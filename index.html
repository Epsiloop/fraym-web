Find a place. Pose. Capture. Remember.

Spots worth the trip

Curated photo spots, each walked and safety-checked on the ground, so you spend time shooting instead of searching.

Walk to the exact spot

Guidance to the photographer position itself, not just the landmark, with distance and heading as you get close.

Frame it right

Rule-of-thirds grid and pose guides on the live camera tell you where to stand, where to look and how to line it up.

Recreate a photo you love

Pick the shot that made you book the trip. Fraym takes you to where it was taken and overlays it so your frame matches.

Memories on the map

Save each photo as a memory pinned to its place on an open OpenStreetMap map, with the date, ready to revisit.

Private by design

Photos stay on your device, location is never stored, and anonymous usage data is one switch away from off.

Built for how you travel

Fraym is a mobile app for iOS and Android. It is in early development, with a pilot in Yerevan first and more cities to follow. Drop us a line and we will email you once, when the beta opens. No spam, no sharing.
